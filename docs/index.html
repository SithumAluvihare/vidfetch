Questions

Short answers to the common ones.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

VidFetch isn't code-signed yet. Signing certificates cost money, and Windows warns about unsigned apps it hasn't seen often. The app is built automatically on GitHub from the public source code, and every release lists its SHA-256 checksum so you can check the file you downloaded.

Which sites does it support?

Anything yt-dlp supports: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Vimeo, X, Reddit and over a thousand more. Paid streaming services that use DRM, such as Netflix or Spotify, are not supported.

A download stopped working. What do I do?

Open Settings (the gear icon), click Update yt-dlp, then restart VidFetch. Sites change often and the engine usually catches up within days. If it still fails, open an issue.

Does it collect any data?

No. VidFetch only talks to the site you download from, to PyPI when you update the engine, and to GitHub once per launch to check for a new version. Your history stays on your PC in %APPDATA%\VidFetch.

How do I uninstall it?

Delete the VidFetch folder. To remove settings and history too, also delete %APPDATA%\VidFetch.

Use it responsibly. Only download videos you own, that are freely licensed, or that you have permission to save. Respect copyright and each site's terms of service. You are responsible for how you use VidFetch.
